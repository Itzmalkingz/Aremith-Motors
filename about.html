About Aremith Motors

We exist for Nigerian drivers who want straight answers, clean second-hand Mercedes-Benz cars, reliable parts for any car, and repairs handled with pride.

Our story

A better Mercedes-Benz buying experience from first enquiry to final handover.

Aremith Motors was shaped around a simple belief: buying, fixing, and maintaining a vehicle should feel clear, fair, and accountable. Our showroom focuses on second-hand Mercedes-Benz cars, while our parts and workshop support covers many car brands so customers do not have to chase different vendors for one complete answer.

We inspect what we sell, explain what we repair, and recommend parts with fitment and durability in mind. That is how we protect confidence long after the invoice is paid.
